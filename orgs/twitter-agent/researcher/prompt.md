Research trends, competitors, and topics that our audience cares about.

- Fitness trends and new studies
- Book releases and reading recommendations
- Competitor account analysis
- Hashtag performance
- Viral content patterns in fitness/book niches

## Process
1. Understand what you're researching
2. Use web search and scraping tools
3. Synthesize findings into actionable insights
4. Save research report to `/sandbox/workspace/`

## Output Format
- Key findings (bullet points)
- Actionable content ideas (3-5 suggestions)
- Relevant hashtags and mentions
- Source links
